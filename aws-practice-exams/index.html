How the practice exams work

  • Timed or untimed. Run a full exam against the clock, or take your time and read as you go.
  • Explanations that teach. Every question says why the right answer is right, why each wrong option is wrong, and links to the AWS documentation.
  • Score by domain. See which exam domains are strong and which need work, instead of one overall percentage.
  • Retest your weak areas. Re-run just the questions you got wrong or flagged.
  • Resume anywhere you left off. An exam in progress is saved in your browser.
  • Private and offline. Your history stays on your device, and the simulator works without a connection.

What these questions are, and are not

The questions are original practice questions generated with AI and written in the style of each exam so that you can test the concepts the exam covers: choosing the right service, spotting the constraint that rules out an option, and trading cost against resilience. They are not official AWS exam content and not leaked questions. If you want to know how the real exam is structured, read the official exam guide on the AWS Certification site, and use these questions to find out what you do not yet know.

How to get the most out of a practice exam

  1. Take one exam cold to find your baseline, without looking anything up.
  2. Read every explanation, including for questions you got right, because the reasoning is the point.
  3. Use the domain breakdown to decide what to study, then retest your weak areas rather than the whole exam.
  4. Build the services in a free-tier account or a simulator so the trade-offs are concrete. The software architecture simulator on this site lets you design and break cloud systems without an account.

Frequently asked questions

Is this really free?

Yes. The practice exams are free, there is no account, no paywall and no email capture. They run in your browser.

Are these real AWS exam questions?

No. They are original, AI-generated practice questions written in the style of each exam, with an explanation for every answer. They are not official AWS questions and not "exam dumps", and they are not affiliated with or endorsed by AWS.

Which AWS certifications can I practise?

Right now the AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate (SAA-C03) with 715 questions and the AWS Certified Developer – Associate (DVA-C02) with 390 questions.

Do the practice exams work offline?

Yes. After the first visit the simulator is cached by your browser, so you can keep practising without a connection, and you can install it to your home screen.

Is my progress saved?

Your history and any exam in progress are stored in your own browser only, so you can resume later on the same device. Nothing is sent to a server.

How is this different from a video course or a paid question bank?

It does not teach from scratch. It is for testing yourself: timed exams, explanations that say why each wrong option is wrong, a score breakdown by exam domain, and a retest of the questions you missed.
