Free AWS practice exams

Practise for the AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate (SAA-C03) and Developer – Associate (DVA-C02) exams with 1,105 scenario questions. Every question explains the right answer and why the other options are wrong, and your score is broken down by exam domain so you know what to study next. No account, and it works offline. The questions are AI-generated from official AWS documentation and are not official exam content.

Practise software architecture and system design

Reading about system design is not the same as being paged for it. Engineering Ops gives you a running cloud architecture, the metrics, logs and traces to investigate it, and a canvas to redesign it. Deploy your change and the simulator explains what happened and why: scaling the app tier that overloads the database, a retry storm, the loss of an availability zone or a whole Region.

  • Software architecture simulator overview and all 14 missions
  • The Database Meltdown: a first incident
  • The Regional Outage: a disaster recovery drill
