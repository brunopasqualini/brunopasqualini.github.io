The Database Meltdown

Flash-sale traffic is 3× normal and checkout is timing out.

  • Incident
  • Intermediate
  • About 25 minutes
  • Free, in your browser

Play this mission

The scenario

It is 14:00 and the “Midsummer Sale” email just landed. Traffic tripled in ten minutes. Checkout latency is climbing, customers see errors, and the on-call channel is on fire. Application servers look idle, yet requests are failing. A teammate has proposed doubling the application replicas “to add capacity”.

Context. ShopStream — consumer marketplace. Flash sale driving 3× normal traffic for the next hour.

What is at stake. Every minute of degraded checkout loses revenue and burns your error budget.

Your brief

  • Restore ≥ 99% availability at peak traffic.
  • Keep p95 latency ≤ 600 ms.
  • Budget $700 / month (simulated estimate).
  • Cached data must stay fresh within 30 seconds.
  • Write a short postmortem when you are done.

What you will learn

  • Capacity analysis and bottleneck identification
  • Query optimisation vs scaling
  • Connection pooling
  • Caching trade-offs
  • Why more app replicas can make things worse

Builds: System design, Site reliability and observability, AWS services, Data and messaging.

How it plays

You are given a running architecture and the evidence a real engineer would have: metrics, logs, traces, cost and recent changes. Form a hypothesis, make a change, deploy it, and read why the system reacted as it did. A verification load test then scores your design and a debrief explains the root cause and the alternatives. Nothing here touches a real cloud account.
